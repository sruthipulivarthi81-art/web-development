📌 Fest Rules & Guidelines

Follow these rules for a safe and enjoyable experience.

  1. Every participant must complete the registration form.
  2. Carry your valid college identity card.
  3. Report to the event venue at least 15 minutes early.
  4. Follow the instructions of coordinators and volunteers.
  5. Maintain discipline and respect all participants.
  6. Use of unfair means in competitions is prohibited.
  7. The organizers' decisions regarding competitions are final.
